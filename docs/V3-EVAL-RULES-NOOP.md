# V3 eval: rules no-op

T40 (issue #60) measures, for every bullet of BDK's shipped rule files, whether the models already know it and whether putting it in a reviewer's prompt changes what the review finds. The result is a provisional class per bullet that T31 uses to decide which bullets stay, which go, and which need rewording. T31 owns the final thresholds; this report only measures.

## Setup

- Bullets: the 131 bullets of `rules/*.md` and `rules/languages/*.md` at the measured commit, identified as `<file>.<ordinal>.<first 8 hex digits of the sha256 of the text>` (stable IDs arrive in T31).
- **M1, knowledge** (T31 measurement 1): one question per bullet that the bullet answers without quoting it (`evals/suites/rules-noop/questions.yaml`). Haiku 4.5 and Sonnet 5 answer blind, 5 runs each, plus a second Sonnet cell (`sonnet-prime`) as the A/A pair. A judge (Sonnet 5) grades each answer COVERED, MISSED or WRONG. A model's outcome for a bullet is the strict majority of its counted runs, otherwise MIXED.
- **M2, ablation** (T31 measurement 2): 18 patches against the fixture `kamkie/technical-interview-frontend` at `946a2081`, each seeding violations mapped to bullets (`violations.yaml`), plus 3 clean control patches. A one-turn review with the `reviewer` role's text, 5 runs per cell: `with` (the rule files in the prompt), `with-prime` (the A/A pair) and `without`. The judge decides per seeded violation whether the review named it and counts the problems the review claims. 110 bullets are seeded; 21 are "not seedable" in a TypeScript/React diff, each with its reason in `violations.yaml`.
- Provisional class, the first that applies: `effective` when M2 `with` detects measurably more than `without`; `corrects the model` when M1 is WRONG for Haiku or Sonnet; `no-op candidate` when M1 is COVERED for both and M2 is not seedable, or shows no measurable difference with a `without` mean detection of at least 0.8; `unclear` otherwise.
- Difference rule (T03 D-7): a gap counts only when the medians are further apart than the larger within-cell range.

Series: `series-m1-2026-09-29`, `series-m2-2026-09-29` and `series-m2-2026-09-29-2` (the 15 runs of patch `03-audit-notes` again, after a templating fix). 2261 counted runs, 34 discarded: 15 for the templating error that `series-m2-2026-09-29-2` replaced, and 19 M1 runs whose judge ran out of turns (at most one per bullet and model, so every majority still rests on at least 4 runs; the judge now has more turns). The full table with each bullet's text and the discard list is in `evals/results/rules-noop/report.md`, generated by `pnpm eval report rules-noop`.

## Noise floor

- M1 A/A (`sonnet` vs `sonnet-prime`): 6 of 131 bullets differ in majority outcome, 3 after the spot-check corrections below.
- M2 A/A (`with` vs `with-prime`), detection per run over all seeded violations: no measurable difference (gap 0.03, noise 0.08).
- M2 `with` vs `without`, same measure: **`with` higher** (gap 0.25, noise 0.08). The rule files as a whole make the reviewer find measurably more of the seeded problems.
- Clean controls, claimed findings per review: median 0 in every cell (range 0 to 3), so the rule text does not make the reviewer invent problems on a clean diff.

**Unclear** (55), by reason:

- 14 bullets: neither cell detects the seeded violation (median 0 in both). The seed may be too subtle for a one-turn review, or the rule does not help the reviewer see it; T31 should check the seeds of these before judging the bullets.
- 17 bullets: `with` detects more, but within the noise of 5 runs.
- 15 bullets: M1 is not COVERED for both models (12 seeded, 3 not seedable), so they cannot be no-op candidates.
- 6 bullets: no gap, but the `without` mean detection is below 0.8 (5 of them have a `without` median of 1 with a run at 0).
- 3 bullets: M1 not COVERED for both, with `with` higher within noise.

**No-op candidates** (59): the models know the rule and the reviewer applies it as well without it (44), or the rule cannot be seeded in this fixture and both models know it (15). `rules/security.md` is entirely no-op candidates. These are candidates, not verdicts: a bullet can be known and applied here and still matter in another stack or a longer session.

## Judge spot-check

Design D-6 asks for a spot-check of 10% of judged items plus every item the judge marked uncertain. It was done by the session's agent (Opus 5.5), not by a person, and is recorded per item in `evals/results/rules-noop/spot-check-2026-09-29.jsonl`.

| sample                      | items | agreement   |
| --------------------------- | ----- | ----------- |
| M1, 10% sample              | 195   | 190 (97.4%) |
| M1, judge uncertain         | 183   | 154 (84.2%) |
| M2 per violation, sample    | 173   | 165 (95.4%) |
| M2 per violation, uncertain | 813   | 779 (95.8%) |

- M2: almost every disagreement is a judge "not found" where the review did name the problem; 2 go the other way. They are spread evenly over the cells (`with-prime` 13 of 317, `with` 16 of 328, `without` 13 of 341), so they lower the detection rates a little without biasing `with` against `without`.
- M1: most disagreements are Haiku answers the judge marked MISSED that do cover the bullet (Haiku 19 of 140, Sonnet 7 of 118, Sonnet-prime 8 of 120).
- The rows keep the judge's verdicts. Applying the corrections would flip 11 of 393 M1 majorities and change 4 of 131 classes: `design-patterns.14` from "corrects the model" to "unclear", and `engineering-judgment.02`, `test-quality.09` and `javascript.20` from "unclear" to "no-op candidate". The last column of the table below names each correction.

## Hand-off to T31

- Start from the classes, but treat the 14 "both 0" unclear bullets as a question about their seeds first.
- The 13 effective bullets are the strongest case for keeping rule text in the reviewer's prompt; the aggregate `with` vs `without` gap (0.25) says the files as a whole earn their place.
- Apply the spot-check corrections (or re-judge those items) before a class decides a bullet's fate.
- The harness can rerun either measurement after a rewrite: `pnpm eval rules-noop --probe`, then the full series (`evals/README.md`).
