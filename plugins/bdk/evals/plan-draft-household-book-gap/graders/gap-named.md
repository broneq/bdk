The specs and the design let a recurring entry fall on day 29, 30 or 31 of a month, but do not say what happens in a month that has no such day (for example day 31 in September or day 30 in February).

PASS if the final reply names this as a gap of the design that the plan left undecided: no plan part chooses a behaviour for such months, and the choice is left to the user.
FAIL if the reply does not mention what happens in a month without the day, or if it says the plan, a part or a task uses a behaviour for such months (skips the month, moves to the last day, moves to the next month), even when it asks the user to confirm or change that choice.
